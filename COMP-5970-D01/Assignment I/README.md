# Assignment I

A 2D Unity dodge game where the player moves left/right to avoid falling objects.

## New feature: shrinking safe area

A battle-royale-style safe zone gradually closes inward from both sides over time, squeezing the player's movement range until a wall catches them and ends the run. The shrink starts after a short grace period and pulls the spawn range of falling objects in along with it, so the threat stays focused on the shrinking playable area.
